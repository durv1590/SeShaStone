# Brand guidelines

The source of truth is [`brand/seshastone-brand-reference.png`](../brand/seshastone-brand-reference.png).

**Positioning:** a modern Indian premium jewellery house where timeless craftsmanship meets contemporary luxury.

**Tagline:** TIMELESS ELEGANCE

> **Logo status: final.** The logo follows Concept 1 ("Luxury SS Monogram") of the brand reference. Every file in `apps/web/public/brand/` is generated by `scripts/brand/generate.mjs` (`npm run brand:generate`). To change the logo, edit the script and regenerate; never edit the generated files by hand.

## Colour

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--ss-ivory` | `#F7F2E8` | Primary background (≈70% neutral) |
| `--ss-charcoal` | `#181818` | Text, header, primary buttons (≈20%) |
| `--ss-black` | `#0D0D0D` | Footer, hero |
| `--ss-champagne` | `#D9AF57` | Accents, logo, CTAs on dark (≈10%) |
| `--ss-gold` | `#C9A24A` | Soft gold accents |
| `--ss-silver` | `#C9C9C9` | Silver jewellery, metallic details |
| `--ss-emerald` | `#0E4A3A` | Premium and festive collections; the premium artificial line |
| `--ss-burgundy` | `#722737` | Bridal and wedding campaigns; errors |

- Gold on ivory fails contrast. For gold text on light backgrounds use `--color-accent-ink` (`#7A5C12`, 5.9:1). Never make whole pages gold.
- Semantic tokens: `--color-background`, `--color-surface`, `--color-text`, `--color-muted`, `--color-primary`, `--color-accent`, `--color-border`, `--color-success`, `--color-error`.

## Typography

- **Cormorant Garamond** (500/600, italic 600): display, headings, product names.
- **Montserrat** (400/500/600): body, navigation, CTAs, prices (tabular figures).
- The type scale lives in `apps/web/app/globals.css` (`--text-display-xl` … `--text-promo`).
- The font files are bundled with the apps (`apps/web/lib/fonts/`, `apps/admin/app/fonts/`, SIL Open Font License), so builds never download from Google Fonts and visitors' browsers never contact Google.

## Logo system

**The mark.** Two Cormorant Garamond italic S letters in Champagne Gold. The second S sits lower-right of the first and passes over it; at the foot of the second S, the lower bowl of the first passes back over it, so the two interlock. A hairline gap separates them wherever they cross, so the mark works on any background colour. The wordmark is "SeSha Stone" in Cormorant Garamond, with "PVT. LTD." and "TIMELESS ELEGANCE" in letter-spaced Montserrat. Everything is converted to vector paths, so no fonts are needed to display it.

**Minimum sizes.** Monogram: 16 px tall on screen (it stays legible as a 32 px favicon). Full horizontal logo: 120 px wide. Below that, use the monogram alone.

Files in `apps/web/public/brand/`:

- **Logo** (`logo/`):
  - `sesha-stone-primary.svg`: champagne monogram with charcoal wordmark, for light backgrounds.
  - `sesha-stone-champagne.svg`: all champagne, for dark backgrounds; used in the header and footer.
  - `sesha-stone-reverse.svg`: ivory.
  - `sesha-stone-monochrome.svg`: black.
  - `sesha-stone-stacked.svg`: the brand-board lockup with tagline.
  - `sesha-stone-secondary.svg`: wordmark only.
  - `sesha-stone-monogram.svg`: the SS symbol.
- **Favicons** (`favicon/`): `favicon.svg`, plus 32, 180 and 512 px PNGs.
- **App icons** (`app-icon/`): `icon-512.png` and `icon-512-emerald.png`.
- **Social** (`social/`): `profile-icon.png` (1080 px).

Usage rules:

- The desktop header uses the full logo; mobile uses the compact monogram.
- Never stretch, rotate, recolour outside the palette, add shadows or effects, or crowd the logo. Keep clear space of at least the height of one "S" around it.

## Photography and campaigns

- **Photography:** real studio photography of the exact piece. Controlled lighting, accurate metal and stone colour, ivory or charcoal backgrounds. No AI-generated or composited jewellery, and no reuse of one image across unrelated products. Artificial pieces must never be styled as gold or diamond.
- **Campaign sizes:** desktop 1920×700, tablet 1280×700, mobile 1080×1350, Instagram 1080×1080, Story 1080×1920, Facebook 1200×628, WhatsApp 1080×1350.
- **Festive direction:**
  - Diwali: warm lamp glow, gold on charcoal.
  - Navratri: refined textile cues.
  - Karwa Chauth: burgundy, ivory and gold, in a bridal editorial style.
  - Akshaya Tritiya: gold and ivory with heritage cues.
  Keep decoration restrained.
- **Packaging:**
  - Primary: matte charcoal with a champagne foil monogram.
  - Secondary: warm ivory with gold.
  - Premium edition: deep emerald with champagne gold.

## Voice

Elegant, warm, confident and precise. No discount-store language, no invented claims, and only verified material descriptions.
